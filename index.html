hacksu

/hak ˈes ˈyü/ noun

the cross-major builder collective. where ideas become real projects.

Build things. Ship fast. Win hackathons.

A student club at Kennesaw State University that helps you enter hackathons and build the AI, programming, marketing, and teamwork skills to win them. Beginners welcome.

01 / what we do

What we do

01

hackathons

our flagship builds. cross-major teams, real problems, working projects by the end of the weekend — not just code, whatever it takes to ship the idea.

02

speaker nights

engineers and recruiters from companies like Anthropic, Adobe, Wells Fargo, and Nutanix come talk shop, share how they got there, and meet the room.

03

resume workshops

hands-on sessions to get your resume, portfolio, and pitch actually ready for internship and co-op season — reviewed by people who’ve hired for the role.

04

build & game nights

low-key sessions to work on your own thing, get unstuck with help from others, or just show up and hang out. no pressure, no agenda.

02 / about

About

about.txt

KSU never had a real home for the people building across majors — CS, engineering, business, design, all of it.

hacksu is that home: a think tank where different skill sets collide to build things that actually ship, and a way to connect current students with alumni and industry so those projects turn into real opportunities.
